Fretless Piano

What if you could play the notes in between the keys on a keyboard? This project is a playable piano with all frequencies available, not just the twelve notes in an octave on a traditional piano. When you click, it displays the frequency ratio to C, which grows logarithmically across the keyboard. (Tone.js)
